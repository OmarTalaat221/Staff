Nour Staff Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nour Staff collects, uses, stores, and protects information when employees, staff members, or administrators use the Nour Staff mobile application, website, management portal, and related internal operational services.

1. Introduction

Nour Staff is an internal operational and staff management platform designed to streamline shift planning, rota schedules, attendance tracking, leave requests, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), training, and payroll logs.

By using the Nour Staff mobile application or web portal, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you are an employee of the company, this policy also complements your employment agreement regarding workspace policy and attendance tracking.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information necessary for internal company operations:

Account and Profile Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Role (e.g., Waiter, Chef, Cashier, Host, Delivery, Manager, Cleaner).
  • Department (e.g., Service, Kitchen, Finance, Logistics, Management).
  • Base salary and payment frequency.
  • Employment details (Join date, status).

Attendance and Location Data

  • Exact clock-in and clock-out timestamps.
  • Geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude GPS coordinates) captured exclusively during checking in and checking out.
  • Late arrival and late departure calculations.

Shifts and Schedule Information

  • Assigned shifts and rota schedules.
  • Shift details, timing, designated break periods, and custom schedule notes.

Leave Requests

  • Requested leave start and end dates.
  • Reason for the leave.
  • Handover notes or instructions for replacement staff.
  • Approval status and administrative feedback.

Operational & Educational Activity

  • Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) view history.
  • Comments and operational feedback notes submitted on SOP steps.
  • Videos watched in the Education Hub, including categories (e.g., Service, Kitchen, Safety, App Guide).

Financial Transfer Records

  • Transaction history for salaries, bonuses, cash advances, deductions, or reimbursements.
  • Payment methods (e.g., Bank Transfer, Cash, E-Wallet) and transaction reference numbers.
  • Receipt and statement attachments uploaded for confirmation.

Device and Security Information

  • Login credentials, session authentication tokens, and login history.
  • Device identities (for account security and prevention of multi-device session sharing).

3. How We Use Information

Nour Staff uses the collected information to support daily operations and management, including:

  • Setting up, managing, and maintaining secure staff accounts.
  • Creating, updating, and publishing shift schedules and rota templates.
  • Tracking workspace attendance and calculating accurate payroll records.
  • Verifying that shift check-ins occur at the proper workplace location.
  • Processing leave requests and coordinating staff coverages.
  • Distributing operational guidelines (SOPs) and tracking training completion.
  • Recording and archiving salary transfers and payroll receipt attachments.
  • Securing the application from unauthorized access or credential sharing.
  • Troubleshooting technical bugs and improving application usability.

4. Location and GPS Data

To ensure correct attendance records and shift verification, Nour Staff records the user's GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) during clock-in and clock-out events.

💡 Note on GPS Collection: Location coordinates are captured only at the exact moments you trigger "Check In" or "Check Out". Nour Staff does not track your device's location in the background or continuously run GPS tracing.

Access to your location permissions is required on your mobile device to perform check-in and check-out functions successfully.

5. Shifts and Attendance

Nour Staff stores all scheduled shifts, actual hours worked, and break times. The system automatically computes late arrival and early/late departure minutes based on scheduled shift times. This data is utilized by the management team to evaluate punctuality, plan labor costs, and compile monthly attendance reports.

6. Leave Requests

When you request a leave of absence, the details you submit (such as the dates, reason, and handover notes) are shared directly with managers and administrators. This information helps coordinate resource planning and ensures that duties are properly handed over to colleagues during your absence.

7. Transfers and Salaries

The system maintains records of financial transfers, including salary payments, bonuses, cash advances, deductions, and reimbursements.

Administrators and staff can upload transaction attachments (e.g., photos or PDFs of receipts) to confirm payment. These files are stored securely in the app database and are only accessible by authorized managers and the individual employee.

8. Operations & Training

The application contains Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and an Education Hub with instructional videos to assist you in your work.

Nour Staff logs progress in training and allows employees to post feedback or operational notes on specific SOP steps. These notes are shared with administrators to enhance training standards and work processes.

9. Data Sharing

Nour Staff is an internal business tool. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal employee information to third parties.

Data is only shared under the following conditions:

  • With management: Authorized administrators and managers have access to reports, attendance logs, and transfer files.
  • Service providers: Trusted server hosting, file storage, and database services that hold data on our behalf, complying with high security standards.
  • Legal compliance: If required by law, regulations, or court orders to protect company rights or address security concerns.

10. Data Security

We deploy strict technical and organizational security measures to protect internal staff data. This includes secure database connections, password hashing, and session authentication tokens.

⚠️ Employees are responsible for keeping their passwords safe and must not share their login credentials. Account sharing can compromise system security and invalidate attendance logs.

11. Data Retention

We retain employee records and logs for as long as needed to fulfill operational requirements, process payroll history, comply with labor laws, and manage corporate tax reporting.

If your employment with the company ends, your profile status will be updated to "Inactive", and data will be archived or deleted in accordance with company policies and applicable regulations.

12. User Rights

As an employee using Nour Staff, you have the right to:

  • View your shift history, attendance logs, and salary transfers directly through the app.
  • Request corrections to incorrect logs, shifts, or profile details from your manager.
  • Submit or delete your pending leave requests.
  • Manage device permissions (such as location permissions) via your device settings. Note that disabling location permissions will prevent you from checking in/out.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our operational procedures, location check-in rules, or security practices. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding how Nour Staff handles your data, please reach out to the management or administration team.
